# Screen 03 — Home

## Purpose
Give users an immediate answer to “What can I cook?” and easy paths into discovery and planning.

## Layout hierarchy
1. Header greeting (generic/time-aware only if desired) and profile/settings shortcut.
2. Search field with placeholder “Search recipes or ingredients”.
3. Prominent Ingredient Finder entry card/action: “What can I cook?”
4. “Explore by category” compact horizontal category tiles.
5. Featured recipe/editorial hero (one strong image, title, short metadata).
6. “Quick & easy” recipe row.
7. “Popular this week” or curated picks.
8. Recently viewed row when history exists.

## Primary actions
- Search → Search Results.
- What can I cook? → Ingredient Finder.
- Category → Search Results with category selected.
- Recipe card → Recipe Details.
- Planner shortcut may be a small contextual link, not a competing hero.

## States
- First launch with curated content.
- Offline with cached content and discreet offline label.
- No recently viewed: hide that section.
- Empty remote catalog: helpful retry/update action.
- Loading: skeleton cards, not a spinner covering the page.

## Ad placement
If ads are enabled, a clearly labelled native ad may appear between discovery sections. Never disguise ads as editorial recommendations. Do not place an ad above the ingredient-finder primary action.

## UI prompt
Design a premium, easy-to-scan recipe app Home screen. Use a warm cream canvas, strong food photography, editorial typography, generous whitespace, and a clear search field near the top. Make “What can I cook?” the standout practical action, followed by visually distinct category tiles and curated recipe rows. Keep the five-item bottom navigation visible with labels. Avoid dashboard metrics, excessive cards, tiny text, and clutter.
