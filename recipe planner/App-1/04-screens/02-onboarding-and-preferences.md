# Screen 02 — Onboarding & Preferences

## Purpose
Collect optional preferences that improve recipe discovery without making setup feel mandatory.

## Layout
- Short welcome headline and one-sentence value proposition.
- Three compact preference sections: dietary preference, favorite cuisines, preferred measurement units.
- Clear “Skip for now” action.
- Primary “Get started” CTA.
- Use selectable chips/cards with text labels; do not require a long form.

## Fields
- Dietary: no preference, vegetarian, vegan, eggetarian, pescatarian (only include supported content values).
- Cuisine interests: multi-select from curated cuisine list.
- Units: metric or US customary.
- Optional notification preference is deferred to Settings; do not ask permission on this screen.

## Behavior
- All choices optional.
- Persist locally.
- Explain that preferences can be changed later.
- Never ask for name, email, phone, or account.
- Complete onboarding and open Home.

## UI prompt
Create a friendly, low-friction onboarding screen for a personal recipe planner. Use a warm editorial style, a compact progress cue, clear selectable preference chips, and a prominent “Get started” button with a visible “Skip for now” option. Keep the screen short and welcoming; no sign-up, login, lengthy questionnaire, or permission popups.
