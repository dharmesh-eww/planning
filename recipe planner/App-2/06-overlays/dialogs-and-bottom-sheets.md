# Dialogs and Bottom Sheets
## Dialog patterns
1 Remove planned meal (explain slot and grocery consequences).
2 Delete grocery list (local list/items removed).
3 Delete collection (recipes remain).
4 Clear checked items.
5 Discard edits.
6 Timer complete (Dismiss / Add one minute).
7 Content update failed (cached recipes remain; Retry).
8 Notification permission rationale (only after user enables feature).
9 Reset local data (list exact data removed).
10 Destructive confirmation (name exact object and outcome).
Use concise copy and two actions where possible.

## Bottom-sheet patterns
1 Recipe quick actions.
2 Recipe filters.
3 Sort options.
4 Cuisine/category/diet selector.
5 Add recipe to planner.
6 Choose planner date and meal.
7 Add grocery item.
8 Edit grocery quantity/unit.
9 Grocery grouping.
10 Ingredient picker.
11 Choose/create collection.
12 Contextual actions.

Sheets have a clear title, close affordance, keyboard/safe-area handling, no stacking, and clear destructive-action separation.
