# Screen 17 — Pantry
Goal: maintain an optional local list of ingredients.
Layout: search/add ingredient; category filters; rows with ingredient, quantity, unit, optional expiry date; user-defined low-stock threshold.
Behavior: local only. Ingredient Finder may preselect pantry items. Never auto-decrement after cooking without confirmation. Expiry labels are reminders, not food-safety guarantees.
UI prompt: Clean pantry screen with searchable ingredient entry, simple category filters, compact editable rows for quantity/unit/date. Lightweight and non-alarmist.
