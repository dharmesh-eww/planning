# Screen 08 — Weekly Planner
## Goal
Make weekly meal planning easy to understand at a glance.
## Layout
Week header with previous/next and date picker; horizontally scrollable day selector; vertically stacked meal slots for breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack; each slot shows recipe summary or Add meal.
## Global behavior
Respect locale week start, date formats, and RTL. Avoid fixed assumptions about Sunday/Monday. Empty slots are clear and tappable. Grocery generation CTA appears after planned meals exist.
## UI generation prompt
Design a minimal weekly meal planner with a locale-aware week strip, clear day selection, and simple meal rows for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks. Empty slots should show an obvious Add meal action. Use restrained borders and small recipe thumbnails. No dense calendar grid.
