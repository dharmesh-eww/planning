# Screen 11 — Grocery List Details

## Layout
List title and date range; grouped item sections; checkboxes; quantity/unit; optional source recipe; add-item action; edit and clear controls.

## Behavior
Check/uncheck items immediately and persist locally. Support rename, quantity edit, group move, and delete. Provide undo for accidental removal where possible. Keep items with incompatible units separate; never infer density for mass-volume conversion.

## Visual style
Simple high-contrast rows on warm surface, thin separators, clear checkboxes. Completed rows may be muted but remain readable.

## Motion
A short checkmark stroke is acceptable; reduced motion switches directly to checked state. No celebratory animation for every item.

## Acceptance
No advertising in this flow. Controls are usable one-handed and with screen readers.
