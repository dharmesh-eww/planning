# Screen 04 — Discover and Search

## Layout
Search field at top; chapter-style category index; cuisine and cooking-time shortcuts; optional recently searched terms; curated recipe shelves. Keep search immediately available.

## Behavior
Support partial text, ingredient names, and localized aliases. Filters can combine: cuisine, category, time, difficulty, dietary preference. Show active filter chips and clear-all. Search history is local and clearable.

## Style and motion
Use restrained index labels, small illustrated category markers, and recipe photography. Search focus uses a brief ink underline; avoid animated background effects.

## Acceptance
Keyboard does not obscure the query/results context. Empty, loading, offline, and error states are defined. Ads, if present, are clearly labelled and visually separate from recipes.
