# Screen 19 — Settings and Content Updates

## Settings
Language, units, first day of week, theme, reduced motion, text-to-speech, cooking screen-awake preference, optional dietary filters, privacy explanation, and app information.

## Content updates
Show current dataset version, latest available version, approximate download size, changelog summary, check-for-updates action, and download/install progress.

## Behavior
Preferences persist locally. Update flow validates manifest and content before atomic activation. Interrupted or invalid updates preserve the current dataset. Cached content remains usable offline.

## Visual style
Organized as clear labelled sections with small chapter markers; controls use standard platform conventions and visible descriptions.

## Motion
Use a restrained progress indicator. Reduced motion replaces animated progress flourishes with a static progress bar and numeric status.

## Acceptance
No account, login, or cloud sync. Clearly explain that recipe content may be downloaded from a public static source while personal data stays on device.
