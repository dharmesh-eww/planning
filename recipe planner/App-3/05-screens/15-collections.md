# Screen 15 — Collections

## Layout
Collection title; create collection action; grid/list of user collections with name, recipe count, and optional cover thumbnail.

## Behavior
Create, rename, reorder, and delete collections. Deleting a collection must not delete its recipes or remove recipes from Saved. Collection names and membership stay local.

## Motion
New collection appears with a subtle fade. Avoid animated book-stack metaphors that slow navigation.

## Acceptance
Empty state explains how collections help organize recipes. Destructive actions require confirmation.
