# Screen 03 — Today Home

## Purpose
Offer a calm daily starting point and one obvious next action.

## Layout
1. Locale-aware date and brief greeting.
2. “Today's table” summary with breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack slots.
3. Add a meal action for the next empty slot.
4. One small recipe suggestion based on saved local interests.
5. Recently viewed or saved recipe strip.
6. Optional editorial content only when available in downloaded data.

## Style
Restrained chapter heading, ruled dividers, and a small hand-drawn ingredient vignette. Avoid scrapbook clutter.

## Behavior
Meal slot opens picker; recipe opens detail. Date labels respect locale and week settings. Empty state offers Plan your week or Find a recipe. Do not invent personalized recommendations without local context.

## Motion
A meal card may settle into place after assignment. No looping hero animation.

## Ads
If used, a clearly labelled ad may appear between discovery blocks only, never between the daily plan summary and direct actions.

## Acceptance
Primary actions visible without scrolling on common phones. Empty/populated states defined. Useful offline.
