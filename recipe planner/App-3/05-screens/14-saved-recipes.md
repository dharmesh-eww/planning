# Screen 14 — Saved Recipes

## Layout
Page title; search within saved recipes; collection/filter chips; saved recipe cards with image, title, and time; empty state with a discover action.

## Behavior
Save/unsave from any recipe detail or card. Keep saved state local. If a recipe is removed from a downloaded content set, preserve a useful unavailable placeholder or metadata record where practical.

## Style
Recipe-index list with small bookmark marks and subtle ruled dividers. Avoid overdecorated shelves.

## Motion
Bookmark confirmation may draw a short check. No large bouncing effects.

## Acceptance
Offline saved recipes remain accessible when their content is cached. Empty state is useful and accessible.
