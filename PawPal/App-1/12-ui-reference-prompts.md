# UI Reference Prompts — PawPal

## Global prompt
Create an original premium pet-care companion Android UI called PawPal. Bright light-only appearance: warm ivory canvas, clean white surfaces, pale oat secondary surfaces, warm charcoal typography, leaf-green primary actions, sunny apricot accents, subtle warm-grey borders, friendly licensed pet photography, generous whitespace, readable editorial typography and calm rounded components. Trustworthy, warm and practical. Clear labels and accessible contrast. Do not use dark surfaces, dark palettes, dark mode or theme switch.

## Screen prompts
1. Home: selected pet, today's care schedule, quick reminder/log actions, reviewed seasonal tip.
2. My Pets: pet cards, add action, species labels, next due task.
3. Pet profile: photo, details, care schedule, recent logs, saved guides.
4. Pet editor: grouped form, optional fields, units, photo picker.
5. Reminder editor: task type, pet, date/time, repeat summary, owner-entered notes.
6. Care log: event timeline and add-entry form.
7. Discover: search, categories, dog/cat filter, editorial cards.
8. Article detail: large pet image, readable sections, reviewer/source metadata, safety callout.
9. Breed library/detail: species switch, practical filters, care descriptors.
10. Saved and settings: collections, units, notifications, content updates, privacy and backup.

## Output
Keep the same light system across screens, overlays and empty states. Use realistic US English content, accessible touch targets, and no medical diagnoses or medicine instructions.
