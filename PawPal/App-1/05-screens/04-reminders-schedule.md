# Screen 04 — Reminders and Care Schedule

## Purpose
Create and manage user-defined care tasks.

## Fields
Pet, title/task type, date, local time, repeat rule, optional owner-entered note, notification toggle.

## Task types
Feeding, water refresh, walk, litter/yard cleanup, grooming, nail trim, dental routine, training, appointment, vaccination follow-up, medication (owner-entered instructions only), and custom.

## Behaviors
Daily, selected weekdays, weekly, monthly, interval, one-time; snooze; mark complete; skip; pause; edit; delete; view history. Summarize recurrence in plain language before saving.

## Safety
Never suggest medication dose, timing, duration, or treatment. For medication reminders, display only information entered by the owner from veterinary instructions.

## Edge cases
Notification denied/revoked, time zone changes, DST gaps/overlaps, device reboot, app update, missed reminder, recurring event on month-end, notification capacity limits.
