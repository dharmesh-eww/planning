# Screen 05 — Care Log and Pet Timeline

## Purpose
Let owners record routine observations without interpreting them medically.

## Entry types
Weight, feeding note, walk/activity, grooming, training, behavior observation, vet visit, medication administered (owner-recorded), and custom note.

## Fields
Pet, event type, date/time, optional numeric value/unit, note, optional local attachment.

## Timeline
Chronological list with filters by event type and date. Weight history chart is optional and descriptive only.

## Interactions
Add, edit, delete, filter, export. Deletion confirmation identifies the pet and entry.

## Safety
No diagnosis, health score, automatic abnormality detection, or medication recommendation. Encourage veterinary consultation for health concerns.
