# Screen 02 — Home

## Purpose
Show the selected pet's upcoming care tasks and the most relevant next actions.

## Layout
- Greeting and pet switcher.
- Selected pet summary with photo, name, species, and quick profile access.
- Today's care schedule grouped by overdue, upcoming, and completed.
- Quick actions: Add Reminder, Log Care, Browse Guides.
- One editorial seasonal tip with source/review context.
- Continue reading and saved quick links.

## Interactions
Complete, snooze, skip, or open a reminder; switch pet; add a task; open article; view all schedule.

## States
No pets (Add Pet CTA), no reminders (create routine prompt), all complete, offline with cached content, stale editorial content, notification permission off, multiple pets.

## Safety
Do not display diagnosis-like health scores or automated risk predictions.
