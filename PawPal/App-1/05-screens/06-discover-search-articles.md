# Screen 06 — Discover, Search, Article Feed and Article Detail

## Discover
Search field; category tiles; dog/cat filters; life-stage filters; featured and recently updated guides; breed directory entry.

## Article feed
Cards show title, summary, species, topic, reading time, review/update date, and image. Sort by relevant, newest, and A–Z. Do not claim popularity without data.

## Article detail
Hero image, title, summary, applicability tags, reviewed-by/source metadata, body sections, step lists, safety callouts, related guides, save and share actions.

## Search
Search title, summary, body, tags, breed, and species within cached content. Debounce local queries; preserve recent searches locally.

## States
Loading from cache, empty category, no results, offline, stale content, article withdrawn, missing image, unsupported content version.

## Editorial safeguards
Prominent safety callout for urgent topics. No user comments or unreviewed submissions.
