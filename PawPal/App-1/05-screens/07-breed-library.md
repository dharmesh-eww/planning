# Screen 07 — Breed Library and Breed Detail

## Directory
Dog/cat toggle; search; filters for size, coat, grooming, activity needs, and living environment. Filters describe tendencies, not guarantees.

## Breed detail
Overview, size range, coat, grooming, exercise/enrichment considerations, temperament descriptors, common care considerations, and related guides.

## Interactions
Save breed, share public profile, compare up to two breeds (optional), assign breed to pet as a best-match label, or select Mixed/Unknown.

## Content rules
Avoid deterministic statements about behavior, health, family suitability, or hypoallergenic guarantees. Include a note that individual animals vary and adoption decisions should consider the specific pet.
