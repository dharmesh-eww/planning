# Assets and Editorial Content

## Asset rules
Use original, commissioned or properly licensed pet photography/illustrations. Track creator, source, license, attribution, dimensions, crop and alt text in an asset manifest. Do not scrape images or reuse copyrighted breed-club assets without permission.

## Image catalog
App icon with recognizable pet/paw motif; neutral dog/cat placeholders; consistent breed hero crops; optimized WebP/JPEG article thumbnails; offline placeholders. Avoid personal pet photos in remote content.

## Launch content target
12–16 categories; 30–40 dog breed profiles and 20–30 cat breed profiles; 50–75 reviewed practical articles; 10–15 positive-reinforcement training programs; curated US safety-resource index.

## Review policy
Veterinary claims reviewed by qualified veterinary professional. Training/behavior material reviewed by qualified professional where appropriate. Each page includes review date, sources and correction route. Time-sensitive content has a review interval.

## Exclusions
No medication dosage tables, treatment protocols, diagnosis trees, unverified home remedies, guaranteed breed temperament claims, or fear-based clickbait.
