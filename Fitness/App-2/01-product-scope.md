# Product Scope — PULSE

Audience: gym regulars, strength-training users, interval-training enthusiasts and motivated beginners who like a focused, energetic interface.

MVP: local onboarding; searchable exercise catalog; program templates; custom workout builder; live session logging; rest/interval timer; workout history; training calendar; goals; body measurements; progress analytics; reminders; export/import; ten-language localization.

Differentiator: a powerful in-session experience with fast set entry, high-contrast metrics, configurable interval blocks and clear performance history.

Out of scope: social competitions, live leaderboards, coaching marketplace, streaming classes, wearable-dependent core flows, diagnosis, calorie prescriptions, accounts and cloud sync.

Starter programs must be clearly labelled as general templates. Do not imply guaranteed strength or body-composition outcomes. Users can adapt, pause or stop a session at any time.
