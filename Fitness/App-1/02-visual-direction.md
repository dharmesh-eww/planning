# Visual Direction — Form & Flow

## Concept
Bright Athletic Editorial: premium training journal, not a noisy gym dashboard.

## Palette
Canvas #F7F8FA; surface #FFFFFF; ink #17202B; secondary #687381; cobalt #315FEA; progress lime #C8F169; success #187A55; caution #B66A22; error #C64F4F. Always pair semantic color with labels/icons.

## Typography
Clean geometric sans for headings, highly legible body face, tabular numerals for sets/reps/durations/measurements. Strong hierarchy; large numbers only for meaningful metrics.

## Layout
8pt spacing; 20–24pt page gutters; restrained 16–22pt card radii. Exercise imagery is instructional, not decorative. Keep active workout controls legible at arm's length. Five-tab bottom navigation with Train emphasized. Avoid dense dashboards, excessive gradients, glassmorphism and meaningless rings.

## Components
Metric tile, weekly activity strip, plan card, exercise row with muscle/equipment tags, set-entry grid, sticky session controls, timer sheet, chart with text summary and empty-state illustration.

## Accessibility
Dynamic type, touch targets, semantic colors, chart legends, reduced motion, screen-reader labels, kg/lb and km/mi preferences, RTL-ready layouts.

## Image direction
Consistent licensed/original exercise photography, accurate posture, inclusive representation, uncluttered backgrounds and consistent camera angles. Never rely on imagery alone for instructions.
