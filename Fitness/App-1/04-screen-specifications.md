# Screen-by-Screen Specifications — Form & Flow

## 01 Welcome and privacy
Brand promise, offline-first and no-account explanation, Continue. Returning users bypass setup.

## 02 Setup preferences
Optional experience, home/gym, available equipment, training days and kg/lb. Skip is always available; settings remain editable.

## 03 Today dashboard
Greeting/date, today's workout hero, Start/Resume, weekly activity strip, next session and concise consistency insight. Empty state offers Browse plans/Create plan.

## 04 Plan library
Search/filter by goal, duration, level, location and equipment. Cards show days/week, session duration and equipment; distinguish bundled from user-created plans.

## 05 Plan detail
Plan summary, level, weekly schedule, exercise preview, equipment, adaptation notes and Start/Save/Customize actions. No guaranteed outcome claims.

## 06 Exercise library
Search, muscle-group chips, equipment and difficulty filters. Rows show thumbnail, name, primary muscle and equipment; provide accessible list alternative.

## 07 Exercise detail
Demonstration image, target muscles, equipment, difficulty, numbered steps, breathing cue, common mistakes, substitutions and safety notes. Defaults are editable suggestions.

## 08 Workout builder
Plan/session name, exercise search, selected list, drag reorder, sets/reps/load/duration/rest configuration, validation and save draft/plan.

## 09 Active workout
Current exercise and instructions, set table, large input controls, completed state, rest action, previous/next, pause and finish. Persist state across backgrounding.

## 10 Rest timer
Large countdown, progress arc, +15/-15 seconds, skip, pause/resume and optional sound/haptic. Announce milestones, not every second.

## 11 Workout completion
Duration, exercises/sets completed, optional perceived effort and notes. Calm completion animation; no shame for partial sessions.

## 12 Workout calendar
Month/week completed, planned and rest days; day selection opens sessions. Include agenda list alternative.

## 13 Progress overview
Range selector; sessions, training time, consistency and performance trends. Charts include units, legend, date range and text summary.

## 14 Body measurements
Manual weight and optional circumference entries, dated history, unit conversion and charts. Hide-sensitive-values option; no diagnosis.

## 15 Goals
Weekly sessions, consistency or user-defined performance target. Progress, edit, pause and delete; avoid prescriptive body-weight goals.

## 16 Workout history
Chronological sessions with date, plan, duration and status; search/filter; useful empty state.

## 17 Session detail
Logged sets/reps/load/duration/notes and records. Allow corrections while preserving timestamp and edit transparency.

## 18 Reminders
Local schedule, day/time, quiet hours and permission education. Graceful denied-permission state.

## 19 Profile and preferences
Units, theme, language, equipment, training days, haptics, sound, reduced motion and privacy.

## 20 Data and backup
Export JSON/CSV; validate import and preview counts; explain merge/replace behavior and sensitive file contents. No cloud upload.

## 21 Language selection
English, Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Marathi, Nepali, Punjabi, Tamil and Telugu in native scripts, no flags, instant preview.

## 22 Exercise content updates
Optional manual GitHub catalog check; version, size and changelog; consent before download; validate before apply; retain bundled fallback and rollback.

## 23 Search
Search exercises, plans and history; categorized results and locally stored recent searches.

## 24 Shared states
No plans/history/measurements, invalid import, storage error, notification denied, reset confirmation and unsaved workout. Localize every state.
