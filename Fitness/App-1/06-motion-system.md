# Motion System — Form & Flow

Motion is energetic but controlled, clarifying state and rewarding consistency without distracting during exercise.

## Timing
Micro feedback 120–180ms; navigation/card 200–280ms; sheet 240–320ms; progress reveal 450–700ms. Ease-out on entry and ease-in on exit; avoid spring overshoot on numeric controls.

## Signature moments
Today hero gently lifts on press; weekly activity bars reveal sequentially; exercise rows animate into builder; set completion uses concise check/fill; rest arc moves smoothly without pulsing; completion uses restrained geometric rays; charts animate once.

## Haptics and sound
Optional light haptic on set/timer completion. Sound user-controlled. Never rely on sound alone.

## Performance/accessibility
Use implicit Flutter animations for simple transitions. Pause offscreen Lottie. Provide still fallbacks and honor reduced motion. Avoid multiple heavy compositions on active workout screen.
