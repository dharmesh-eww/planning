# Product Scope — Form & Flow

## Audience
Beginners building consistency, home-workout users, gym members tracking loads, and people who want a private training log.

## Core jobs
1. Choose or create a weekly routine.
2. Understand exercise technique.
3. Record sets, reps, duration, distance and optional load.
4. Follow sessions with rest intervals.
5. Review consistency and performance trends.
6. Set goals and reminders.

## MVP
Onboarding preferences; searchable exercise library; sample and custom plans; workout builder; active workout with set logging and rest timer; completion summary; workout history; calendar; goals; body measurements; progress charts; local reminders; localization; backup/export/import.

## Later
Supersets, circuits, personal records, exercise substitutions, custom exercises, home-screen widgets and opt-in Health Connect/Apple Health integration.

## Out of scope
Social feed, public leaderboards, coaching marketplace, streaming video, live coaching, medical recommendations, mandatory account, remote user profiles and cloud sync.

## Content policy
Bundle a reviewed starter library. GitHub may host versioned public exercise and workout-plan catalogs. Each exercise needs stable ID, muscles, equipment, difficulty, setup, steps, common mistakes and safety notes. Avoid guaranteed weight-loss or muscle-gain claims.

## Success measures
Plan created, workouts started/completed per week, repeat logging, crash-free sessions, reliable persistence and reminder quality.
