# Fitness — App-1: Form & Flow

**Concept:** Bright Athletic Editorial — a clean, energetic fitness companion for everyday training.

An offline-first Flutter fitness planner. Users choose or build routines, follow exercise instructions, record sets and reps, and review progress without an account or developer-operated backend.

## Constraints
- No custom server, API, Firebase, mandatory account or cloud sync.
- SQLite (sqflite) is the source of truth for personal data.
- Core features work offline; baseline exercise content and images ship in the app.
- Optional GitHub-hosted versioned JSON distributes public exercise and plan catalogs only; it never receives personal data.
- User-controlled export/import is the backup mechanism.

## Visual identity
Warm-white canvas, ink typography, vivid cobalt actions, citrus-lime progress accents, cool-gray surfaces, editorial exercise imagery and crisp charts.
