# Screen-by-Screen Specifications — GROVE

## 01 Welcome
Soft botanical mark, friendly promise and offline/no-account statement. Continue with a static fallback for animation.

## 02 Your movement preferences
Optional focus (strength, mobility, walking, general fitness), available equipment, weekly availability and preferred units. No body data required.

## 03 My Day
Date greeting, today's movement card, weekly garden progress, gentle reminder, recent journal note and quick start. Rest day is a positive state, not an empty failure.

## 04 Move library
Browse strength, mobility, bodyweight, walking and recovery-friendly general movement categories. Filter by equipment, duration and level.

## 05 Routine collections
Curated starter routines with days/week, duration, equipment and level. Clearly label general-purpose plans and allow adaptation.

## 06 Routine detail
Editorial overview, schedule, movement list, equipment, variations, save and customize actions.

## 07 Exercise detail
Illustrated movement, setup, steps, breathing, common mistakes, easier/harder variations and safety notes. Offer text-first instructions.

## 08 Create a routine
Name, weekly schedule, add/reorder movement, configure sets/reps/time/rest, save locally. Keep advanced fields optional.

## 09 Session preparation
Preview planned movements, estimated duration, optional warm-up and Start. Let user remove or substitute movements.

## 10 Active movement
One movement at a time, illustration/instructions, sets/reps/time inputs, completion state, pause, skip and finish. Controls remain calm and legible.

## 11 Breathing/rest timer
Soft expanding ring, countdown, +15/-15, skip, pause and optional haptic. Never force breath holds or medical breathing guidance.

## 12 Session reflection
Duration, movements completed, optional effort, mood/comfort note and free text. Avoid interpreting mood as health diagnosis.

## 13 Movement calendar
Scheduled, completed and rest days with a list alternative. Users can reschedule without penalty.

## 14 Journal timeline
Session cards with date, routine, duration, notes and filters. Search personal notes locally.

## 15 Growth overview
Consistency, active days, total movement time and selected performance trends. Use friendly text summaries and clear units.

## 16 Goals and intentions
Set a weekly movement intention or user-defined target. Pause, edit and celebrate progress without pressure.

## 17 Personal measurements (optional)
Manual entries for weight or body measurements only if user chooses. Hide values, explain units, show dated history. No BMI diagnosis or body scoring.

## 18 Reminders
Local notifications by day/time, quiet hours, gentle copy and permission education. No guilt language.

## 19 Preferences
Theme, language, units, movement interests, equipment, haptics, sounds, reduced motion and privacy.

## 20 Backup and privacy
Export/import local JSON/CSV, validate and preview before import, explain sensitive data, delete local data with confirmation.

## 21 Language picker
English, Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Marathi, Nepali, Punjabi, Tamil and Telugu in native scripts; instant preview; no flags.

## 22 Content updates
Optional manual GitHub content check, pack version and changelog, user consent, validation and rollback.

## 23 Search
Search movement library, routines and journal; local recent searches and clear history.

## 24 Shared states
Rest day, no routines, no journal entries, timer recovery, invalid backup, reminder denied and unsaved edits; fully localized.
