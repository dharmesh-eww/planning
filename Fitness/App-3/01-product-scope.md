# Product Scope — GROVE

Audience: beginners, people returning to movement, home exercisers, walking/mobility enthusiasts and users who prefer a calm habit-focused experience.

MVP: gentle onboarding; goal and availability setup; movement library; beginner-friendly plan templates; custom weekly routine; workout/session logging; interval/rest timer; calendar; habit streaks without punitive language; measurements optional; progress journal; local reminders; offline backup/export; localization.

Differentiator: blend structured training with mobility, bodyweight, walking and recovery-day routines. Focus on showing consistency and personal notes, not competitive metrics.

Out of scope: social comparison, weight-loss promises, medical/rehabilitation plans, diet prescriptions, remote coaching, live video, accounts and cloud sync.

Content must clearly distinguish general exercise ideas from individualized professional advice. Users can substitute, skip or stop any movement.
