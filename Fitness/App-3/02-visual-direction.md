# Visual Direction — GROVE

## Concept
Modern Botanical Journal: warm, human, tactile and calm, without looking rustic or dated.

## Palette
Paper #F7F3EA; surface #FFFCF6; forest #244A3B; sage #A8BBA2; soft mint #DCE8D8; terracotta #C9785A; muted plum #78677E; ink #28342E. Use color semantically and retain accessible contrast.

## Typography
Editorial serif for occasional page titles paired with a friendly sans for controls/body. Tabular numerals for timer and measurements. Avoid overly decorative fonts in data-heavy screens.

## Layout
Generous whitespace, paper-like cards, rounded corners, subtle section dividers, botanical line illustrations and organic progress paths. Large clear primary action; five-tab navigation. No cluttered dashboard or faux-paper textures that reduce legibility.

## Components
Daily movement card, weekly habit garden, illustrated exercise tile, session journal prompt, soft progress path, calendar dots, optional measurement timeline and calm reminder sheet.

## Imagery
Original vector illustrations with diverse body shapes and accurate movement silhouettes. Use photography only when technique clarity benefits. No body transformation comparisons.

## Accessibility
High contrast on warm backgrounds, large text, clear controls, screen reader labels, reduced motion, text descriptions for illustrations and full keyboard/focus support where applicable.
