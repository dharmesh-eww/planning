# Roadmap and Acceptance

## Release 1 — Daily astrology companion
Onboarding/sign selection, Today, 12 sign profiles, daily horoscope categories, weekly/monthly readings, favorites, sharing, Explore learning library, offline cache, privacy/disclaimer and local reminders.

## Release 1.1 — Deeper engagement
Compatibility content, lunar calendar with sourced precomputed events, journal prompts and private local journal, reading history, export/import.

## Release 1.2 — Content and quality
Expanded learning library, additional share-card templates, more robust editorial calendar, content correction/withdrawal workflow, improved accessibility and localization readiness.

## Later candidates (validate first)
Optional paid ad-free version, widgets, more editorial series, Spanish US localization. Birth charts/ascendant/planetary transits require a separately validated ephemeris engine, explicit scope, precise birth data consent and privacy review; they are not part of this plan.

## Acceptance
User can select a sign and read a dated horoscope without account; static content is never misrepresented as individualized birth-chart computation; personal data stays local; GitHub requests are limited/read-only; dates and timezones are correct; disclaimers are clear; compatibility is non-deterministic; light-only design; ads do not interrupt journaling or essential readings.
