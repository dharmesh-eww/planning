# Monetization and Advertising

## Principle
Ads should fit editorial browsing and never interrupt a private reflection or a reading interaction.

## AdMob placements
- Native ad between organic cards in Explore/learning feed and optionally zodiac directory.
- Banner only on low-risk browsing screens with reserved layout space.
- Interstitial disabled for MVP; if later enabled, natural navigation break only, frequency-capped, never on app launch, sign selection, journal save or back navigation.
- Rewarded ad only for optional decorative share-card themes or nonessential bonus editorial extras. Core readings and sign profiles remain available without an ad.

## Exclusions
No ads in onboarding, journal editor, export/import, settings/privacy, notification permission explanation or disclaimer screens. Never imply a reading is more accurate after an ad.

## Consent and privacy
Use Google UMP where applicable; honor region-specific consent and non-personalized ad availability. Never use birth date, journal text, selected sign, relationship details or reminder data for targeting. Disclose SDK data processing in store privacy forms.

## Measurement
Evaluate placement impressions, accidental taps, retention and crashes using aggregate events only where disclosed. No personal journal or birth details in analytics.

## Release
Use test ad units in development; verify production IDs, UMP flow, app-ads.txt where applicable, store privacy disclosures and frequency caps.
