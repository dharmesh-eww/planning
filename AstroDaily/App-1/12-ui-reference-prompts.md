# UI Reference Prompts — AstroDaily

## Global prompt
Create an original premium zodiac and horoscope Android app called AstroDaily. Use a refined bright light-only editorial aesthetic: soft daylight cream canvas, white cards, pale lavender-grey surfaces, ink-plum typography, clear indigo actions, restrained champagne accents, soft coral details, subtle constellation line art, simple zodiac glyphs, generous whitespace and highly readable text. The visual identity should feel calm, optimistic and modern—not a dark galaxy or mystical night-sky interface. No dark surfaces, dark palettes, dark mode or theme switch.

## Screen prompts
1. Today: selected zodiac sign, date, daily reading categories, reflection prompt and save/share actions.
2. Zodiac directory: 12 sign cards with accessible names and date ranges.
3. Sign profile: symbol, element, modality, ruling planet, traditional overview and related readings.
4. Period readings: daily/weekly/monthly date ranges and category tabs.
5. Compatibility: two sign selectors, explanatory themes and conversation prompts; no score gauge.
6. Moon calendar: month timeline with labelled phases, local event times and source details.
7. Explore: astrology basics, elements, modalities, symbols and learning cards.
8. Journal: private reflection prompt and clean writing surface.
9. Saved: favorite readings, signs, articles and history.
10. Settings: selected sign, reminders, content updates, privacy and export.

## Output
Maintain consistent light surfaces, contrast and typography across all screens, dialogs and share cards. Use realistic US English. No deterministic prediction copy, scientific claims, birth-chart calculation UI or dark cosmic backgrounds.
