# Accessibility and Localization

## Accessibility
- Aim for 48dp touch targets and support Android font scaling.
- Screen-reader labels include full sign names; zodiac glyphs alone are insufficient.
- Lunar phase icons and charts include text labels and summaries.
- Ensure reading text has comfortable line length, spacing and contrast.
- Logical focus order, keyboard visibility, reduced-motion behavior and semantic headings.
- Never rely on color alone for category, selected sign or event type.

## Localization
Initial release en-US. Externalize all strings and editorial content. Format dates and times with locale/timezone libraries. Store content dates in stable ISO format and UTC timestamps with source timezone metadata. Future locale candidates should be selected by audience evidence and professionally reviewed.

## Content language quality
Horoscopes should be original, varied, non-alarmist and non-deterministic. Avoid absolute predictions, fear-based language and stereotypes. Translation must preserve disclaimers and not strengthen certainty.
