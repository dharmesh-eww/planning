# Screen 03 — Daily, Weekly and Monthly Readings

## Reading categories
General, love/relationships, career/work, wellbeing/reflection. Tabs or chips; category can be disabled in preferences.

## Reading content
Title, sign, period type, explicit start/end dates, publication date, reading text, reflection prompt, optional editorial lucky color/number, disclaimer, save/share.

## Behavior
Switch date or period; handle missing content without fabricating readings. If only cached previous reading exists, label its date clearly. Do not imply content is individualized by birth chart.

## States
Loading, available, not yet published, missing, stale, offline, withdrawn, unsupported version.
