# Screen 01 — Splash, Onboarding and Today

## Splash
Light canvas, AstroDaily wordmark, concise initialization. Load local preferences/cache first; never wait for GitHub.

## Onboarding
- Welcome and entertainment/self-reflection positioning.
- Choose zodiac sign manually or optionally enter month/day.
- Full birth date is optional and stored locally only.
- Cusp-date explanation and manual override.
- Explain notifications and local privacy; defer permission request until reminder opt-in.
- No account, email or phone collection.

## Today layout
- Selected sign and date.
- Daily reading card with general/love/career/wellbeing categories.
- Reading period and published date.
- Reflection prompt.
- Save/share actions.
- Weekly/monthly entry points and content freshness indicator.

## States
First launch, returning user, no sign, missing today's reading, offline cached reading, stale content, delayed publication, notification permission off.
