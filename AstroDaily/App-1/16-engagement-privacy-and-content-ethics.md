# AstroDaily — Engagement, Privacy and Content Ethics

## Engagement features
- Daily check-in, optional reading streak, favorites and weekly reflection summary.
- Streak is informational, user-disableable and resettable; no punitive loss messaging.
- Weekly recap uses only local reading/journal metadata and never interprets mood or mental health.
- No public profiles, comments, social feed, chat, user horoscope submissions or cloud sync.

## Notifications
- Daily reminder local time, selected weekdays, weekly reminder, lunar-event reminder and optional favorite-sign reminder.
- Per-notification toggle, quiet hours, preview, disable all and permission status.
- Avoid alarming, deterministic or manipulative notification copy.
- Do not put birth date, journal text or sensitive relationship content in notification payloads.

## Journal privacy
- Journal is local-only; optionally protect access using device authentication if supported.
- Explicit delete-one/delete-all and export.
- Share/export preview must show exactly which fields will leave the app.
- No automatic cloud/OS backup claims; explain Android backup behavior and provide a clear local export path.
- Never include journal text in analytics, crash logs or ad SDKs.

## Advertising boundaries
- Native ads only in Explore/learning feeds and optionally zodiac directory after organic content.
- No ad in the middle of a horoscope body, journal, compatibility result, lunar event detail or share-card preview.
- Interstitial disabled in MVP; never app launch, back navigation, sign selection or reading completion.
- No ad-based claims of more accurate predictions or privileged destiny insights.
- Do not target using selected sign, birth date, journal, relationship preferences or notification settings.

## Content ethics
- Clearly identify the app as Western tropical sun-sign entertainment unless otherwise stated.
- No certainty language (“will happen”, “guaranteed”, “destined”) or fear-based predictions.
- No medical, financial, legal, fertility, safety or crisis guidance.
- Compatibility is reflective, not a measure of relationship quality.
- Lunar/retrograde material must have sourced dates and transparent provenance.
- Include a report/correction route for inaccurate, offensive or repetitive content.
