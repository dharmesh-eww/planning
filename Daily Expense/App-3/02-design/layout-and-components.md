# Layout and Component Language

## Layout rhythm
Use a 4/8 pt spacing rhythm, generous 20–24 pt card padding, 18–24 pt corner radii, and clear section breaks. The home screen can use an editorial split: greeting and balance at top, compact weekly cash-flow card, budget snapshot, recent transactions, and goal card.

## Components
- Primary button: forest fill, white label, pill/rounded rectangle.
- Secondary action: pale sage fill or outlined forest.
- Cards: warm white with fine warm-gray border and subtle shadow.
- Chips: pale sage with dark forest text.
- Transaction row: category illustration/icon, title and date, right-aligned amount with explicit type label.
- Progress: rounded bar with exact amount and percentage.
- Bottom navigation: warm paper surface, forest active icon/label, simple line icons.

## Responsive behavior
Support compact phones, large phones and tablets. Avoid fixed-height content; support large text and long translated strings. Use safe areas and scrollable layouts.

## Light/dark
Light theme is signature. Dark theme uses deep green-charcoal surfaces and softened botanical accents; preserve semantic colors and contrast.
