# Visual Direction — Bloom Ledger

## Concept: The Gentle Money Journal
A light, nature-inspired editorial interface with paper texture cues, botanical line art, rounded paper cards, forest-green anchors, and warm fruit/earth accents. Avoid the dark neon dashboard aesthetic of App-2 and the minimal neutral utility approach of App-1.

## Palette
Warm paper #F8F5EA; card white #FFFEF8; forest #174B3A; leaf #3D8B68; sage #A9C6A5; mint #DCEFE1; marigold #F2B84B; peach #F2A17F; berry #C86E69; ink #24342D; muted #78847B.

Use forest for primary actions and navigation, green for positive/income, terracotta/berry for expense, marigold for attention. Never rely on color alone. Provide a dark theme with equivalent contrast.

## Typography
Friendly editorial display face for headings paired with highly legible sans body text. Tabular numerals for amounts. Use script-appropriate fonts for all locales. Avoid overly playful handwritten body fonts.

## Layout
Asymmetric but orderly editorial composition, generous whitespace, soft rounded cards, small botanical motifs, section headers with subtle illustration, compact bottom navigation. Home uses a welcoming greeting and a clear balance summary, not a giant dark hero panel.

## Illustrations
Flat vector botanical and everyday-life objects: sprout wallet, coin jar, receipt, shopping basket, goal notebook, calendar leaf. Keep forms simple and scalable; no stock photo dependency.

## Charts
Soft filled area, rounded bars, ring breakdowns with labels and exact numbers. Include text summary and accessible data table. No misleading 3D charts.

## Avoid
Dark neon, glassmorphism, excessive gradients, childish mascots, gamification, confetti, noisy paper textures, illegible script fonts, and overdecorated backgrounds.
