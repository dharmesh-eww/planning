# Product Requirements — Bloom Ledger

## Vision
A gentle, clear money journal that helps users build awareness and steady habits without shame, pressure, or gamification.

## Scope
Home overview; transaction CRUD; income/expense/transfer types; accounts; categories; budgets; reports; calendar; recurring rules; savings goals; search/filter; local export/import/backup; optional Google Sheets; ten-language picker.

## Non-functional
Offline-first, SQLite authoritative, privacy-first, exact money arithmetic, accessible contrast, text scaling, RTL, screen readers, reduced motion, responsive layouts and smooth low-end Android performance.

## Exclusions
No bank scraping, open banking, SMS parsing by default, investment advice, credit scoring, financial product sales, social competition, mandatory account, backend, Firebase, or developer cloud storage.

## Acceptance
User selects language, creates account, records expense, sets budget, and reviews reports offline. No Sheets authorization or data transfer occurs without explicit user action.
