# Screen 16 — Settings and Localization

## Settings
Language; preferred currency; date format; first day of week; theme; number format; privacy/hide balances; reminders; reduced motion; backup/sync preferences; app information.

## Language selection
Support English, Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Marathi, Nepali, Punjabi, Tamil, and Telugu. Display language names in their own scripts and avoid flags.

## Behavior
Changing language updates all app-owned strings immediately without changing stored transaction values, category IDs, dates, or user-created names. Currency preference does not rewrite historical transaction currency.

## Acceptance
No hardcoded user-facing strings. All empty/error/validation/permission/sync messages and accessibility labels are localized.
