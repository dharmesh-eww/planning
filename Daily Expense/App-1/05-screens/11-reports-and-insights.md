# Screen 11 — Reports and Insights

## Report types
Income vs expense over time; spending by category; account totals; budget comparison; top merchants; custom date range.

## Layout
Period and account/currency filters; chart; text summary; category/transaction drill-down.

## Behavior
All reports calculate from SQLite. Never sum unlike currencies. Clearly label the date range and currency. If data is insufficient, show a helpful empty state rather than a misleading chart.

## Accessibility
Charts require a textual summary and accessible data labels. Use patterns/labels in addition to color.
