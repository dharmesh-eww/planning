# Screen 10 — Budget Details and Editor

## Fields
Budget name (optional), amount limit, currency, period type, start/end date, optional category, rollover policy.

## Details
Show spent, remaining, percentage, included transactions, and edit/pause/delete actions.

## Behavior
Budget calculations derive from eligible local expense transactions. Avoid double-counting transfers. Refund treatment and rollover behavior must be explicit and testable.

## Acceptance
Changing a budget never changes transactions. Local updates work offline.
