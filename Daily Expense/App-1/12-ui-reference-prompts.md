# UI Reference Prompts — Daily Expense App-1

Create original mobile UI mockups in the Clear Ledger visual direction. Do not copy any existing finance app.

## Global prompt
A calm, trustworthy personal expense and budget tracker mobile UI. Neutral light background, white surfaces, deep charcoal/navy typography, restrained teal for income/positive values, muted terracotta for expenses, amber budget warnings, clean sans-serif typography, tabular financial numerals, generous spacing, simple outlined icons, clear data hierarchy, high contrast, accessible touch targets. Modern, minimal, practical, not gamified. No excessive gradients, glassmorphism, decorative coins, or bank branding.

## Screen prompts
1. Home dashboard with income/expense/net summary, budget progress, recent transactions, and clear Add Expense/Add Income actions.
2. Transaction list with date groups, category icons, search, and filter chips.
3. Add expense form with large amount field, category, account, date, and note.
4. Add income form with distinct income state and amount entry.
5. Budget overview with total and category budgets, progress, spent and remaining labels.
6. Reports with simple labelled charts and readable text summaries.
7. Accounts screen with cash and bank accounts in separate currency contexts.
8. Google Sheets connection screen explaining optional authorization and selected spreadsheet.
9. Language picker showing ten language names in their own scripts, no flags.
10. Settings screen with language, currency, theme, privacy, and sync options.

## Output requirements
Use consistent device frame and spacing. Include dark theme, RTL layout, and large-text variants. Keep all sample text legible and realistic. Do not include actual bank logos or personal financial information.
