# Dialogs and Bottom Sheets

## Dialogs
1. Confirm delete transaction.
2. Confirm delete/archive account.
3. Confirm delete category and choose reassignment.
4. Confirm delete budget.
5. Confirm clear/reset local data.
6. Confirm import merge/replace.
7. Explain Google permission request.
8. Confirm first spreadsheet export/sync.
9. Explain sync conflict and resolution.
10. Explain revoked permission or unavailable spreadsheet.
11. Confirm disconnect Google Sheets.
12. Explain currency mismatch before transfer.
13. Confirm delete recurring rule.
14. Explain reminder permission limitation.

## Bottom sheets
1. Choose transaction type.
2. Choose category/subcategory.
3. Choose account.
4. Choose transaction date/time.
5. Choose transaction filters.
6. Choose budget period.
7. Choose chart/report period.
8. Add transaction note/tag.
9. Choose recurring frequency.
10. Select Google spreadsheet.
11. Choose Google sync mode.
12. Resolve conflicting record fields.

## Rules
All content is localized. Use explicit action labels and plain language. Destructive actions require confirmation. Never put OAuth consent details only in a tooltip. Sheets expose accessible title, description, and focus order. Respect reduced motion.
